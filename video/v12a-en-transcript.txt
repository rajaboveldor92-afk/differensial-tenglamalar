Systems: matrix form and elimination

[0:00] In real processes several quantities influence each other: the temperatures of two rooms, numbers of predators and prey, susceptible and infected computers. In this lesson we write such systems in matrix form and solve them by elimination.

[0:17] In a normal system the derivative of each unknown is expressed through the others. In the linear case we collect the coefficients into a matrix A and write X prime equals A X plus F. An n-th order equation also becomes a system: take y, y prime and so on as new unknowns.

[0:38] Example: x prime equals two x plus y, y prime equals three x plus four y. From the first equation, y equals x prime minus two x. Differentiate the first equation and replace y prime using the second one.
